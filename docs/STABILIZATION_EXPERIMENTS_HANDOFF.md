## Current objective and state

The goal is smooth, flowy post-Gyroflow footage. Small residual vibration, shake, or judder remains noticeable and is not an acceptable end state. The user says the footage was recorded at 100 fps and viewed on a 165 Hz display, and strongly suspects bursts of corrupt gyro from a poorly isolated or overly sensitive gyro. Treat playback cadence as a low-priority explanation; continue to focus on the gyro/repair path unless new evidence points elsewhere.

Production remains unchanged: reviewed M2 default with optional M4. No research candidate has been promoted. Preserve all existing working-tree changes and source/reference clips. Rebuild both shipping release binaries only if production core code changes.

The accepted research baseline is the fixed edge-offset repair with 0.19 s ramps, original 0.20 s severe padding, original 30 deg/s rebase threshold, and 1.5 deg/s offset decay. It materially reduced the 0060 judder and received positive validation on 0071 and 0073. The latest visually supported candidate adds a gated, localized post-render rotation correction to the v1 translation correction for the 141.253–144.451 s burst in 0073. The user reports that both close-up and full-frame views look better, with a small amount of vibration/judder remaining. This is the strongest positive localized visual result, but its gate has only been tried on known windows; validate it on unseen bursts/clips before integration. Do not blindly increase correction strength.

The separate gap2-plus-edge-offset candidate was described by the user as a possible tiny improvement, with late-clip judder essentially identical. It remains a weak, separate research candidate. It is not part of the latest selective-render candidate. Other windows for both candidates lack a user verdict unless stated below.

## Rulings that should carry forward

- Keep the reviewed production M2/M4 behavior unchanged. There is no promoted research candidate.
- Use fixed edge offsets + 0.19 s ramps + original 0.20 s padding + 30 deg/s rebase gate + 1.5 deg/s decay as the strongest validated research baseline.
- Keep render-selective-v1 as the strongest localized positive candidate, layered on the v1 translation correction. Preserve its comparison and gate unchanged for unseen-event validation. Its successful event was already known during exploration, so the current event-selection evidence is not an independent generalization result.
- Keep the gap2-plus-edge candidate and subset ensemble distinct; the reported benefit is weak, and neither resolves the visible late judder.
- Retire blanket localized output smoothing. Do not repeat zero decay, broad forced rebasing, partner suppression, or similar parameter sweeps without a new discriminating hypothesis.
- Do not let optical/image-motion proxy gains overrule a human verdict. The user's smooth/flowy visual criterion is the acceptance gate; held-out, clean, and additional-window checks protect against overfitting.
- Keep playback cadence low priority per the user's 165 Hz / 100 fps observation. Continue to examine burst contamination and residual handling in the gyro/repair path.
- Lens/scaling and source geometric-estimator expansion were deferred. Their reports remain part of the record, but they are not the current investigation priority.
